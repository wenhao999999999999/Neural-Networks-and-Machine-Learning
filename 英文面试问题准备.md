### 介绍项目中遇到的困难

### Describe a challenging project you worked on. How did you overcome difficulties?

​	One challenging project I worked on was developing a pineapple flowering-inducing robot. During the physical testing phase（/feɪz/）, we faced a critical issue: the robot couldn't accurately（/ˈækjərətli/） spray the medicine onto the pineapple's core. The end effector, which was supposed to reach the position identified by the depth camera, consistently missed the actual core location.

​	To troubleshoot（/ˈtrʌblʃuːt/）, we analyzed the entire process and realized the problem stemmed from the end effector trying to position itself while the robot's base was still moving—this caused a slight but significant positional 偏差 (deviation).

​	Our solution was to add a real-time position compensation（/ˌkɒmpenˈseɪʃ(ə)n/） algorithm. We calculated the typical movement offset based on the base's speed and adjusted the target position accordingly. After implementing this compensation, the end effector successfully aligned with the core, and the medicine was applied accurately（/ˈækjərətli/）. This experience taught me the importance of debugging step-by-step and adapting to dynamic variables in hardware-software integration.
